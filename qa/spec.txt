הוא יכול לעבוד עם המערכות שכבר יש לי?
ברוב המקרים כן. בשיחת ההיכרות נבדוק אילו מערכות יש לך ואיך מתחברים אליהן.

English
How is an agent different from regular software?
Software does what you click. The agent we build for you completes an entire task on its own, from start to finish,
and only brings you what needs your decision.
Can it work with the systems I already have?
In most cases, yes. In the intro call we check which systems you have and how to connect to them.
What if it makes a mistake?
You decide in advance what it may do on its own and what needs your approval, and you can see every action it
takes.
Will my team need to learn something new?
Almost nothing. The agent works inside the tools your team already knows.
